# Batch: 1000+ World OSINT Collectors (all-country coverage)

Added **1,058** new self-registering `source_def` collectors — every one a real
RSS/Atom endpoint routed through the shared `rss_collect` toolkit, so each feed
item lands as an FTS-indexed, entity/alert-hookable intel row. All `free_tier=1`,
scheduled, auto-globbed by the Makefile, generated by
`collectors/gen_world_sources.py`. IDs verified unique against the existing 705.

## Composition (reliability-weighted)

| File | Count | What |
|------|-------|------|
| `gnews_world_top.c`        | 142 | Google News **national top-headlines** editions — one per country worldwide |
| `gnews_world_topics_1.c`   | 270 | Google News topic sections (World / National / Business) for 90 priority countries |
| `gnews_world_topics_2.c`   | 270 | Google News topic sections (Technology / Science / Health) for the same 90 |
| `gnews_osint_monitors.c`   |  70 | Google News **keyword monitors** — 50 global OSINT terms (cyberattack, missile test, sanctions, undersea cable, coup, disease outbreak…) + 20 per-hotspot regional queries (Taiwan Strait, Red Sea, Sahel, Gaza front, etc.) |
| `reddit_world_geo.c`       | 142 | Reddit `/r/<sub>/.rss` — country, city, and OSINT/defense/cyber subreddits (community signal + local reporting) |
| `world_outlets_1/2.c`      | 114 | Curated **native national outlets & agencies** (TASS, RT, Global Times, Le Monde, Der Spiegel, ANSA, Dawn, Anadolu, Rappler, VnExpress, Folha, Clarín, Arab News, Al-Monitor, Balkan Insight, Eurasianet, Irrawaddy…) — provider diversity beyond the aggregators |
| `world_cert_advisories.c`  |  30 | National **CERT/CSIRT** advisory feeds (CERT-FR, CERT-PL, NCSC-NL, CERT-In, ACSC-AU, CCCS-CA, CERT-UA, KrCERT, CSA-SG…) |
| `osint_thematic_global.c`  |  20 | Thematic domains — maritime (gCaptain, Splash247), aviation (Aviation Herald), space (SpaceNews), energy (OilPrice), health (CIDRAP, STAT), rights/conflict (HRW, Amnesty, Crisis Group, ACLED, Lawfare) |

**Total: 1,058 sources.** Deterministic providers (Google News + Reddit) are
~750 of these — the reliable backbone. The remaining ~308 are diverse curated
feeds and hotspot monitors.

## Design notes
- **Google News editions** use the stable public RSS scheme
  (`/rss`, `/rss/headlines/section/topic/<TOPIC>`, `/rss/search?q=`) with the
  correct `hl`/`gl`/`ceid` per country — genuinely one of the highest-yield free
  global news-monitoring surfaces for OSINT.
- **Monitors** poll every 30 min for near-real-time alerting; editions/outlets
  hourly.
- **Graceful failure**: if any upstream URL rotates or an edition doesn't exist
  for a small country, `rss_collect` returns `-1` and that single source shows as
  *errored* in the Source Dashboard — it never emits bad rows and never blocks
  its neighbours.
- **Verification**: all 9 files pass `cc -fsyntax-only -Wall -Wextra` cleanly
  against the real `source.h` ABI; symbol and ID uniqueness verified in the
  generator. A full link needs `libcurl` dev headers (absent in the build
  sandbox). Live feed reachability could not be checked here because the sandbox
  egress proxy blocks `news.google.com` / `reddit.com` / most outlets by policy —
  these resolve normally in a standard deployment. Expect a minority of the ~114
  hand-curated outlet URLs to need small adjustments in your network; the
  dashboard's errored-source list makes them trivial to spot and fix.

## Regenerating / tuning
`collectors/gen_world_sources.py` holds the country table, topic list, monitor
queries, subreddit list, and curated-outlet/CERT tables. Edit those tables and
re-run to add/adjust sources.
