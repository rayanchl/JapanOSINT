## How to use this with your platform

- **Dedupe** against your registry IDs (`./native/bin/japanosint --list-sources`). Many `cam-*`, `censys*`, `shodan*`, academic, aviation and maritime entries here likely overlap what you already collect.
- **Prioritize by collector value**: free + API-first + high-signal (e.g. crt.sh, GDELT, OpenSanctions, abuse.ch family, ADS-B.fi/adsb.lol, OpenCelliD, urlscan, OpenCorporates, GLEIF) are the cheapest high-yield additions.
- **Key/no-key**: `[API]` = usually free/anon, `[API/key]` = free-tier key, `[commercial]`/`[licensed]` = paid, `[OSS]` = self-host, `[ref]` = technique/pointer not a single endpoint.
- **Legality**: several categories (breach data, face search, darkweb, scraping social platforms) carry ToS/consent/jurisdiction constraints. Gate them behind your tenant's authorization model and honor each provider's terms.
