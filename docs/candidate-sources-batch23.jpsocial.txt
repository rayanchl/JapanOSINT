# Japanese rather than Japanese-speaking, the developer and writing platforms where a
# person's technical and professional history actually lives, and the bookmarking layer

#
# Every row below returned parsed records over the wire during discovery. Rows carrying an
# entity token are searches and were additionally checked against an entity that cannot
# exist (house rule 4d) - a row whose filter turned out to be ignored is not here.
#
# NOT HERE, and why: the 2ch-family boards (5ch, Shitaraba, open2ch, machi.to). 15 machi.to
# boards and 3 open2ch boards were discovered live and parse cleanly, but their subject.txt
# is Shift_JIS and lib/hpengine.c has no transcode on its fetch path - lib/feedlib.c has one,
# gated on a .jp host, and hpengine does not use it. Registering them would store mojibake
# titles. 5ch and Shitaraba answer 403 to this host outright. Both are recorded as findings
# rather than shipped as rows.
